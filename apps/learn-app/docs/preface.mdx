# Preface

Welcome to **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics: From Digital Intelligence to Embodied Robots**!

## About This Book

This comprehensive textbook guides you through the exciting journey from traditional digital AI to embodied physical AI and humanoid robotics. Across 26 chapters organized into 7 parts, you'll learn:

- 🤖 **Foundations of Physical AI** - Understanding the transition from digital to embodied intelligence
- 🔧 **ROS 2 Fundamentals** - Master the Robot Operating System 2
- 🌐 **Simulation** - Virtual environments with Gazebo and Unity
- 🚀 **NVIDIA Isaac Platform** - Advanced robotics simulation and AI
- 🦾 **Humanoid Development** - Bipedal locomotion and manipulation
- 👁️ **Vision-Language-Action Models** - Cutting-edge VLA models
- 🎯 **Capstone Project** - Build an integrated humanoid assistant

## Learning Approach

Each chapter follows a structured format:

1. **Learning Objectives** - What you'll learn
2. **Prerequisites** - Required background knowledge
3. **Core Concepts** - Theoretical foundations
4. **Hands-On Tutorial** - Practical, runnable code examples
5. **Summary** - Key takeaways
6. **Exercises** - Practice problems to reinforce learning

## Code Examples

All code examples are:
- ✅ Tested and runnable
- ✅ Fully explained with comments
- ✅ Available for download
- ✅ Compatible with current versions (ROS 2 Humble/Iron, Isaac Sim 2023.1.0+)

## Target Audience

This book is designed for:

- **Students** entering robotics and physical AI
- **Software Engineers** transitioning to robotics
- **Researchers** exploring embodied AI
- **Hobbyists** building robot projects

**No prior robotics experience required!** We define all technical terms and build knowledge progressively.

## Course Structure

### Part 1: Foundations of Physical AI (Chapters 1-4)
Understanding the paradigm shift from digital to physical intelligence.

### Part 2: ROS 2 Fundamentals (Chapters 5-9)
Nodes, topics, services, actions, and the ROS 2 ecosystem.

### Part 3: Simulation (Chapters 10-13)
Virtual environments with Gazebo and Unity for safe development.

### Part 4: NVIDIA Isaac Platform (Chapters 14-18)
Isaac Sim, Isaac Gym, and Isaac Cortex for advanced robotics.

### Part 5: Humanoid Development (Chapters 19-22)
Bipedal locomotion, balance, and manipulation.

### Part 6: Vision-Language-Action Models (Chapters 23-25)
RT-X, OpenVLA, and multimodal robot learning.

### Part 7: Capstone Project (Chapter 26)
Integrate everything into a functional humanoid assistant.

## How to Use This Book

1. **Read Sequentially** - Chapters build on each other
2. **Run the Code** - Every example is meant to be executed
3. **Complete Exercises** - Reinforce your understanding
